Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ASA Small Tribes Monitor Discord bot ("the Bot") handles information when it is used on a Discord server.

1. Information We Collect

The Bot may process limited Discord information necessary to operate its commands and monitoring features. This may include Discord user IDs, Discord server (guild) IDs, channel information, and command-related information.

The Bot may also store information about monitored game servers, such as server addresses, ports, server names, map names, player counts, online status, ping measurements, errors, and monitoring timestamps.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate, maintain, and improve the Bot's monitoring features. Game-server information is used to display server status and maintain monitoring history.

The Bot is intended to provide network/server-status monitoring and does not intentionally access game-process memory, modify game clients, or collect files from users' computers.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information processed by the Bot is not intentionally shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the Bot or comply with applicable law.

4. Data Storage

Monitoring data may be stored in a local database operated by the Bot's owner. The database may contain monitored server information and historical monitoring snapshots.

Data retention may depend on the Bot owner's configuration and database management.

5. Discord

The Bot operates through Discord's platform and is subject to Discord's own terms and policies. The Bot does not control information that Discord independently collects or processes.

6. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe the Bot has collected information that should not have been collected, please contact the Bot owner so the information can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

7. Data Deletion Requests

If you have a request concerning personal information processed by this Bot, contact the Bot owner. Please include enough information to identify the relevant Discord account or data without sending passwords, tokens, or other authentication credentials.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information stored by the Bot. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot's features or data practices change. The date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently updated.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Bot owner through the Discord server where the Bot is operated.
